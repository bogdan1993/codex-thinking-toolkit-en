# assumptions

## Activation and scope
Use for `$assumptions`, the conversational alias `/ASSUMPTIONS`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Plan, argument or problem; optionally evidence and constraints. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Identify explicit and implicit assumptions. Classify information as Confirmed facts, Assumptions, Estimates, Unknowns or Conditions requiring validation. For each consequential assumption, explain its effect on the conclusion and a proportionate way to validate it. Do not turn a plausible estimate into a fact.

## Output
Assumptions, classifications, consequences and validation options. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$assumptions Identify the assumptions behind this budget.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
