# content-suite

## Activation and scope
Use for `$content-suite`, the conversational alias `/CONTENT-SUITE`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Idea or material; optionally channels, purpose, audience, brand and lengths. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Establish the shared message and the role of each channel. Adapt angle, structure, opening, length, tone, call to action and format separately; omit a call to action when appropriate to the purpose. Do not mechanically duplicate the same text or invent experiences and data for variety. If channels are unspecified, propose a proportionate selection and label it. For a personal voice, use available samples or an author profile.

## Output
A coherent content package with distinct channel adaptations. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$content-suite Create a LinkedIn post and a short video script about feedback.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
