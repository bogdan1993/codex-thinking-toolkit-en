# red-team

## Activation and scope
Use for `$red-team`, the conversational alias `/RED-TEAM`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Solution or assumption; optionally domain, boundaries and resources. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Look for plausible failure scenarios, vulnerabilities, hidden risks, edge cases and unintended consequences. Explain the mechanism and the conditions that invalidate a recommendation. Separate demonstrated risks from hypothetical scenarios. Suggest mitigation, early warning signs and residual risk. An adversarial analysis does not authorize intrusive tests or external actions.

## Output
Plausible failure scenarios and proportionate mitigations. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$red-team Stress-test this plan if the main supplier becomes unavailable.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
