# master

## Activation and scope
Use for `$master`, the conversational alias `/MASTER`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Goal and deliverable; optionally constraints, deadline, resources and requested skills. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Identify the outcome and acceptance criteria. Select only the necessary stages and dependencies. Read relevant available skills once per task; arrange research, verification, analysis/comparison, decision and implementation where useful. Reuse evidence, avoid recursive orchestration and flag incompatible requirements. Carry out only the work authorized by the request.

## Output
The requested deliverable, reasons for decisions, and verification status. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$master $analyze Prepare a decision from the attached data without external research.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
