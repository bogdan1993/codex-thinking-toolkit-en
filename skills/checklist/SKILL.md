# checklist

## Activation and scope
Use for `$checklist`, the conversational alias `/CHECKLIST`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Requirements or process; optionally stage, owner and required evidence. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Extract requirements without adding unsolicited obligations. Phrase each item so an evaluator can determine whether it is complete and what evidence is required. Separate optional conditions from requirements. Do not check items off without evidence or confuse file existence with acceptance.

## Output
A verifiable checklist with completion criteria. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$checklist Create acceptance checks for a PDF containing diacritics and tables.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
