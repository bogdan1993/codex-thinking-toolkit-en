# brand

## Activation and scope
Use for `$brand`, the conversational alias `/BRAND`, or requests clearly matching the description. A slash alias is text, not a registered native slash command. A term appearing only in quoted material does not select this workflow.

## Inputs
Material and brand documentation; optionally channel, audience and purpose. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Identify the available official brand documentation and version. Apply relevant visual, verbal and strategic rules and check consistency. Do not invent official colors, fonts, logos or rules. Distinguish extracted values, explicit rules and proposals. If sources are missing, ask for them or label the result as a proposal. For specialized outputs such as PDFs, use an available suitable workflow. When personal voice is requested, use the supplied profile or samples and flag material conflicts with brand requirements.

## Output
Consistent material with documented deviations and limitations. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`$brand Apply the attached brand guide to this presentation while preserving its data.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
