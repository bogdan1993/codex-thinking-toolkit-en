# /MASTER

# master


## Inputs
Goal and deliverable; optionally constraints, deadline, resources and requested skills. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Identify the outcome and acceptance criteria. Select only the necessary stages and dependencies. Read relevant modes in this library once per task; arrange research, verification, analysis/comparison, decision and implementation where useful. Reuse evidence, avoid recursive orchestration and flag incompatible requirements. Carry out only the work authorized by the request.

## Output
The requested deliverable, reasons for decisions, and verification status. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

# /VERIFY

# verify


## Inputs
Claim, calculation or material; optionally evidence and reference date. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Break the material into verifiable claims. Recalculate quantities with tools when useful; check units, denominators and rounding. Use one verdict per claim: CONFIRMED, PARTIALLY CONFIRMED, UNCONFIRMED, FALSE or UNVERIFIABLE. UNCONFIRMED means insufficient evidence; FALSE requires a demonstrated contradiction; UNVERIFIABLE identifies a concrete access or method limitation. Explain evidence, sources and limits. Missing evidence alone does not make a claim false.

## Output
Claims, verdicts, supporting reasons and limits. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

# /SOURCE-CHECK

# source-check


## Inputs
Sources, links or sourced claims; optionally purpose and time period. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Inspect authorship, provenance, method, relevant expertise, date and connection to the claim. Prefer available primary sources. Identify documented financial or institutional interests and dependencies between sources: repeated copies of one press release are not independent confirmation. Flag weak secondary evidence and unsupported claims. Popularity does not establish validity. Distinguish documented interests from possible conflicts.

Assessment of each source and its effect on the conclusion. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.
