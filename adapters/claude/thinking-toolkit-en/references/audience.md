# audience


## Inputs
Content or strategy and audience; optionally purpose, context and objections. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Identify the audience's needs, experience, language, context and objections. Adapt explanation depth, examples and structure. If the audience is undefined, ask for relevant information or state the assumption used. Do not alter facts for appeal or treat stereotypes as established characteristics.

## Output
Audience-appropriate content or strategy with relevant assumptions. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`/AUDIENCE Explain two-factor authentication to people without technical experience.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
