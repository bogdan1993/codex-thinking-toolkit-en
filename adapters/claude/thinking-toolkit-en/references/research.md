# research


## Inputs
Research question; optionally domain, time period, source constraints and depth. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Define the question and what evidence would change the conclusion. Search supplied materials and relevant sources; use the web when requested or when current information is required. Read sources before citing them. Compare independent evidence and explain disagreements. Distinguish documented findings from interpretation, assumptions and estimates. An inaccessible source or unread search result is not evidence; do not invent citations or investigations.

## Output
Supported findings with source links, uncertainty and evidence gaps. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`/RESEARCH Compare the evidence in these two attached reports.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
