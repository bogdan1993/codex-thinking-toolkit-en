# rewrite


## Inputs
Existing text; optionally objective, tone, audience, length and language. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Preserve meaning and accurate information. Adapt structure and wording to purpose, tone and audience. Do not introduce unjustified factual changes; flag ambiguities that change the meaning. Ask for the original if missing. For a personal voice, use supplied samples or an available author profile without inventing experience or requiring a particular external skill.

## Output
Copy-ready rewritten text. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`/REWRITE Rewrite this email in a warm, concise tone: [text].`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
