# analyze


## Inputs
Problem, material or data; optionally objective and assumptions. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Separate verifiable observations from interpretation. Break down the problem, trace relationships and possible mechanisms, and identify patterns and anomalies. Treat causes as tentative until evidence supports them; correlation alone does not establish causation. Keep conclusions proportionate to the evidence and explain consequential uncertainties.

## Output
Structured analysis, implications and uncertainty. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`/ANALYZE Explain what can be inferred from these retention data.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
