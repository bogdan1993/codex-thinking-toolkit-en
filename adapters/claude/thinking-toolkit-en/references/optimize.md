# optimize


## Inputs
EXISTING material or system; optionally objective, metrics and constraints. Natural-language parameters are supported.

## Workflow
Inspect the baseline and identify what works. Improve structure, clarity, efficiency, persuasion or user experience according to the objective. Avoid unnecessary rebuilding and gratuitous changes. Do not add unverified facts. Explain the main changes and their intended effect; an expected benefit is not a measured result. If the baseline is missing, ask for it rather than inventing an original.

## Output
Improved material or system with the main changes and their purpose. Follow the requested format and language; use English when no language preference is available.

## Missing information and composition
Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; continue independent work and label assumptions. Do not claim actions, access, checks or results that did not occur. Combine relevant skills in a logical order, reuse evidence and avoid loops. Research externally only when needed or requested. Activating a skill does not itself authorize sending, publishing, installing or modifying external resources; follow the scope authorized by the user.

## Example
`/OPTIMIZE Simplify the attached text while preserving its message and data.`

## Acceptance
Meet the workflow and requested output, preserve constraints, and distinguish facts, interpretations, estimates and recommendations where relevant. Verify the result, not merely the existence of a file.
